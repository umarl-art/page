Im hated by all grades
By the end of the day
I will be gutted
On the cosmic plate.
Inviting Evil
I will sacrifice myself
To flames, give my soul
To the Lord of Darkness.
With razor deep cuts
On my chest, I will
Lose my life with grace.

𝔓𝔞𝔩𝔢 𝔄𝔯𝔦𝔰𝔱𝔬𝔠𝔯𝔞𝔱


I've seen you walking in the cold,
I bet your parents were incestous.
I know you'll die young,
your coffin tall and thin
And sent to hell by the outrageous sin.
Ough! Pale Aristocrat, you stab yourself blade deep,
punishing yourself your father was your mother's twin.
Pale aristocrat, don't you see your beauty?
Your paper fragile heart troubled pounding.
Pale Aristocrat, your beauty is so rare.
But I really wish you were never there.
Because you are too fragile.
Ough! This story's tragic.
Dear young person... How unfortunate it is to be a pure blue blood son?
Only you knew, and the knowledge washes away,
as your eyes lose the spark of life in them.
